Hertape didn't start with a plan. It started with a product I wanted to exist and a stubborn belief that I could figure out the rest as I went. That worked — until it didn't. Here's the honest version of what I'd change.

1. **I'd build the system before I needed it.** I spent years running operations in my head and on WhatsApp. By the time I built Hertape Smart App — a proper internal OS for the team — we were already behind. Systems should be infrastructure, not a rescue mission.

2. **I'd collect emails from day one.** We had customers before we had a list. Every sale that happened before we had CRM set up was a relationship we couldn't build on. Email is the only channel you actually own.

3. **I'd charge more, earlier.** Underpricing is a confidence problem wearing a strategy costume. The customers who push back on price are rarely the ones who become your best customers anyway.

4. **I'd document the brand before building the product.** We had a clear aesthetic in our heads for years before we wrote it down. That meant every new team member had to reverse-engineer the vibe from existing work. A proper brand guide would have saved us months.

5. **I'd build in public.** The brands that grew fastest around us weren't necessarily the best — they were the most visible about their process. People don't just buy products. They buy into stories. I started too late.

None of this is regret. Hertape is what it is because of everything I learned the hard way. But if you're earlier in the journey than I was — maybe some of this saves you a lap.
